10 · Claude Design direction

Rules to preserve during exploration.

Non-negotiable
One action blue#1673E8 is for calls to action, focus and intentional navigation—not decorative fill.
Evidence before theatreAvailability, price and market signals always carry source, freshness or uncertainty.
Four-pixel rhythmUse 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24 and 32px increments. Tight, calm, never cramped.
Green is semanticUse green only for confirmed availability or a genuinely positive operational status.
Light canvas, real contrastCool white surfaces, dark ink copy and legible borders. Avoid low-contrast glossy cards.
No decorative mysteryEvery mark, button and label must have a purpose. No Sparkles motif or generic AI copy.
