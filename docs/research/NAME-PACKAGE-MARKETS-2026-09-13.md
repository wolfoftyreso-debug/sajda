# Name-package market sources — 2026-09-13

## Decision and verification boundary

Recommend a **38-country manual-check catalog**: the existing USA and 27 EU countries, plus Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, United Kingdom (`GB`), Switzerland, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, and Japan. This is a source-directory expansion, not 38-country automated screening or legal clearance. The ten additional countries are reviewed below; this document does not individually re-audit all existing USA/EU national sources.

Official public guidance, source landing pages, and API documentation were reviewed on 2026-09-13. No candidate names were submitted, registers scraped, datasets downloaded, accounts created, or API integrations exercised. A documented API is an integration candidate, not evidence that this product has credentials, a production licence, complete coverage, or a successful check.

`reviewed_on` records this catalog review. It must never become a candidate's `checked_at`. Selecting a country or opening a link does not populate `checked_markets`; that remains empty until a real, scoped observation is recorded. Company-name, trademark, and domain observations must remain separate.

## Geographic scope that must stay explicit

- BRIS connects company registers in the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Norway. Its stated scope includes limited-liability companies and their branches, not every business form or every possible trading name. It is a useful manual starting point, not proof of comprehensive national coverage. [European e-Justice: Find a company](https://e-justice.europa.eu/topics/registers-business-insolvency-land/business-registers-search-company-eu/general-information-find-company_en), [national register directory](https://e-justice.europa.eu/topics/registers-business-insolvency-land/business-registers-eu-countries_en).
- An EU trademark covers the EU, not all of Europe or the EEA. National and EU rights coexist; retain national-office checks and separate entries for the additional European countries. Do not treat a Switzerland search as a Liechtenstein trademark check. [EUIPO: Where to register](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/trade-marks/before-applying/where-to-register), [EU trademark regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2017/1001/oj/eng).
- WIPO is a supplementary multi-office source, not a universal clearance register. Its collections and update timing depend on participating offices; WIPO also recommends national-office searches. [WIPO FAQ](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/faqs_branddb), [current collection coverage](https://branddb.wipo.int/en/coverage).

### Liechtenstein service-status caveat

The government announced that the commercial register was temporarily offline on 2026-08-10, with extracts available through the Office of Justice. Its 2026-08-31 notice described phased restoration of government systems. These dated notices justify a fallback/contact link and an availability check; they do **not** establish that the register is still offline on the review date. [10 August notice](https://www.regierung.li/medienportal-medium/16444/234723/0/medienmitteilung), [31 August restoration notice](https://www.regierung.li/medienportal-medium/16444/234821/0/medienmitteilung).

## Hard terms boundary: WIPO is manual-only here

The Global Brand Database FAQ explicitly prohibits automatic querying and automatic search/download. Its terms also prohibit automated queries, scraping, and bulk acquisition/redistribution. Consequently, do not send candidate names to WIPO through agents, browser automation, direct undocumented requests, or a scraper. A numerical excessive-use threshold is not permission for slower automated querying. [WIPO FAQ](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/faqs_branddb), [WIPO terms and conditions](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/terms_and_conditions).

The FAQ distinguishes separately obtainable Madrid-system data from the Global Brand Database. Any future licensed data procurement is a separate terms-and-coverage review, not an exception inferred for the public search site. [WIPO FAQ](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/faqs_branddb).

## Practical rollout recommendation

1. Ship the 38-country manual source directory with per-country gaps, not an “available in 38 countries” name verdict. Retain separate company and trademark actions, and preserve the selected markets in export/machine output without claiming they were checked.
2. Prioritize a later integration feasibility review for UK and Norway company APIs, then NZBN/IPONZ and IP Australia's trademark API. This order is an engineering judgment based on the documented search interfaces above, not a licence approval or a statement that they provide legal clearance.
3. Evaluate Switzerland provisioned data, Canada federal/API plus trademark bulk data, Singapore ACRA, and Japan NTA as scoped evidence sources. Explicitly budget for partial geographic/entity coverage, update lag, credentials, reuse rights, and language handling. Keep Iceland pending access review and Liechtenstein manual-only for now.
4. Require each automated connector to define permitted use, authentication, quotas, retention/redistribution, covered entities/classes/statuses, pagination/truncation, upstream observation time, and failure behavior before activation. An empty result, stale snapshot, portal outage, or unsupported market must stay unknown, not become a clearance claim.
5. Expand further only after the same official-source and terms review. WIPO/TMview can help humans discover participating collections, but neither a long country list nor a manual-search URL proves a candidate was screened in those countries.

Company registration, trademark search, and domain availability answer different questions. Similarity, goods/services, local naming rules, unregistered rights, and professional legal assessment can remain material even when a literal search has no matches. The catalog must continue to describe research steps rather than legal approval. [UKIPO search limitations](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/unacceptable-trade-marks), [IPI search limitations](https://www.ige.ch/en/services/digital-resources/databases-and-directories/swissreg/trade-mark-database).
